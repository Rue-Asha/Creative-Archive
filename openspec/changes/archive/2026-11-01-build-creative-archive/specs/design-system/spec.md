## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mockup photos
The repo SHALL keep about ten CC0 photos, downloaded once from the Openverse catalogue (`license=cc0`), in
`fixtures/mockups/`, each listed in `fixtures/mockups/CREDITS.txt` as `file | source URL | author: … | licence: …`; a
needed shape that wasn't found SHALL be generated by `scripts/synthetic-photo.mjs` and credited as generated. The
photos SHALL be used only by `/__mockups/*` and e2e fixtures and SHALL NOT ship in the build or the tarball.

#### Scenario: Every mockup photo is credited
- **WHEN** the image files in `fixtures/mockups/` are compared with `CREDITS.txt`
- **THEN** there are at least 8 images, each has a line with a source URL or `generated`, an author and a licence matching `CC0`, `Public Domain` or `generated (scripts/synthetic-photo.mjs)`
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Missing photo shape is generated
- **WHEN** `node scripts/synthetic-photo.mjs <name> <width> <height>` runs
- **THEN** it writes `fixtures/mockups/<name>.jpg` with exactly that size, and sharp reads it as JPEG
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Mockup photos stay out of the product
- **WHEN** `static/` and `build/client` are listed after `npm run build`
- **THEN** neither contains a `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif` file other than icons under 10 KB, and the tarball's top level holds no `fixtures/` (harness: "Package produces a verifiable tarball")
- **proof:** unit

### Requirement: Tokens and worlds
All colours, radii, blur, shadows, spacing, durations and easings SHALL be CSS custom properties in
`src/lib/styles/tokens.css`, mirrored for tests in `tokens.ts`. A world SHALL be selected by exactly three attributes,
`data-scheme` (light | dark, a complete ground/surface/ink/glass/line/scrim/shadow set), `data-accent` (an accent
set) and `data-font` (a type pairing, see Fonts), and `worlds.ts` SHALL map every world (home, music, animanga, books,
fashion) to one scheme and accent and hold one app-wide pairing (`FONT`), so that changing a world's look is a change
to that map only.

#### Scenario: Every scheme and accent defines the full token set
- **WHEN** `tokens.ts` is read
- **THEN** the light and dark schemes define the same token names, and every accent defines `accent`, `accent-ink`, `accent-soft` and `accent-wash` for both schemes
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Switching a world is a data change
- **WHEN** `worldAttrs('music', { scheme: 'dark', accent: <any music candidate> })` is applied to the mockup page wrapper
- **THEN** the computed `--ground`, `--ink` and `--accent` on the wrapper equal the dark scheme's and that accent's values from `tokens.ts`, and the wrapper carries `data-font` with the app-wide pairing
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Text contrast meets AA in every world
- **WHEN** every text/background pair the components use is checked in both schemes with every accent candidate
- **THEN** body-text pairs reach at least 4.5:1 and large display text at least 3:1
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Text over photos stays legible
- **WHEN** ink on glass and on-photo text over the scrim are composited over a pure black and a pure white photo in both schemes
- **THEN** each reaches at least 4.5:1
- **proof:** unit

### Requirement: Fonts
The app SHALL self-host three OFL type pairings as woff2 in `static/fonts/` with their licence texts, declared with
`@font-face` in `global.css`, and SHALL load no resource from another origin. A pairing is one token block selected
by `data-font` (`a` | `b` | `c`) that sets `--font-display`, `--font-text` and the display weight, tracking, leading,
scale and accent-word style. The app uses one pairing everywhere (`FONT` in `worlds.ts`, picked at the world
checkpoint): `a`, Bricolage Grotesque for display and Figtree for text; `app.html` preloads that pairing's two files.
The other two (`b` Syne + Manrope, `c` Bodoni Moda + Hanken Grotesk) stay selectable with `?font=` on the mockups.

#### Scenario: Display and text fonts load from the app
- **WHEN** a mockup page has loaded with each pairing (`?font=a|b|c`)
- **THEN** `document.fonts` reports that pairing's display and text families loaded and every font request went to the app's own origin
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Type pairings are token blocks selected by data-font
- **WHEN** `tokens.ts`, `tokens.css` and `global.css` are read
- **THEN** pairings `a`, `b` and `c` each define the same full set of type tokens, `tokens.css` has a `[data-font='<name>']` block with exactly those values (the app-wide pairing's block also on `:root`), and every family they name has an `@font-face` and its OFL text in `static/fonts/`
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Pages load no external resources
- **WHEN** each mockup page loads
- **THEN** no request leaves the app's origin
- **proof:** e2e

### Requirement: Shared components
`src/lib/ui/` SHALL provide GlassPanel, PillNav, ArrowButton, RoundNav, Button (dark pill CTA with trailing arrow),
Chip, Card (Swap pattern), StatusPill (split pill), StarRating (half stars, display and input), ProgressControl,
Counter, Eyebrow, ImagePlaceholder (accent tint + initials) and Collage, with the props in design.md → Contracts,
rendered on `/__mockups/components`.

#### Scenario: Card follows the Swap layout
- **WHEN** `/__mockups/components` shows a Card with a photo, chip, title and arrow
- **THEN** the photo fills the card, the chip sits in the top-left quarter, the title in the bottom-left quarter and the round arrow in the bottom-right quarter
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Star rating shows half stars
- **WHEN** a StarRating in display mode gets 3.5
- **THEN** three stars are full, the fourth is half filled, the fifth empty, and its accessible name says "3.5 of 5"
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Status split-pill opens its choices
- **WHEN** the StatusPill's chevron half is activated on the components page
- **THEN** a menu lists every option label and choosing one shows it as the pill's label
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Placeholder shows a tint and initials
- **WHEN** `initials` is called with "Frieren Beyond Journey's End", "ästhetik archive" and "村上春樹"
- **THEN** it returns "FB", "ÄA" and "村上"
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Controls are at least 44px on phone
- **WHEN** the components page and each mockup are measured on the phone project
- **THEN** every button, link-button, chip and input is at least 44×44 px
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Components match the visual brief
- **WHEN** Rue looks at `/__mockups/components` in both schemes
- **THEN** glass, pills, arrows, Swap card, split-pill, stars and placeholder read as the brief and the inspiration describe
- **proof:** manual (visual judgement at the ⏸ checkpoint)

### Requirement: Mockups and the world checkpoint
`/__mockups/[world]/[scheme]` SHALL render Home and one domain landing page per domain in a light and a dark variant
with the mockup photos (accent switchable with `?accent=<candidate>`), at phone and desktop layouts per the brief,
answering 404 without the test flag. `npm run shots:mockups` SHALL write a screenshot per world, scheme and project.
The build SHALL pause after these mockups until Rue has picked scheme and accent per world, and the pick SHALL be
written into design.md → World pick before any unit that builds themed UI starts.

#### Scenario: Mockups render every world in light and dark
- **WHEN** `/__mockups/<world>/<scheme>` is opened for each of the five worlds and both schemes
- **THEN** each renders with `data-scheme` equal to the scheme, a photo hero and at least one shelf of cards
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Mockup routes are hidden without the test flag
- **WHEN** the mockup layout's load runs while `CREATIVE_ARCHIVE_TEST` is not `1`
- **THEN** it answers 404
- **proof:** unit

#### Scenario: Mockup screenshots are produced for both viewports
- **WHEN** `npm run shots:mockups` runs
- **THEN** `test-results/shots/mockups/<project>-<world>-<scheme>.png` exists for 2 projects × 5 worlds × 2 schemes
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Mockups share one alignment rule
- **WHEN** every mockup is measured at 390 and 1280 px
- **THEN** the page header and every section heading start at the content column's left edge (16 px on phone, column edge of a centred ≤1200 px column on desktop)
- **proof:** e2e

#### Scenario: Rue picks a world per domain
- **WHEN** Rue has looked at the mockups and screenshots
- **THEN** she names light or dark and an accent for home, music, animanga, books and fashion, and the orchestrator writes it into design.md → World pick and checks task 4.7
- **proof:** manual (taste decision; the ⏸ checkpoint)

#### Scenario: Requested changes revise the mockups
- **WHEN** Rue asks for a variant to change at the checkpoint
- **THEN** the design unit revises the mockups on its branch and the pause continues until she picks
- **proof:** manual (taste iteration; the ⏸ checkpoint)
